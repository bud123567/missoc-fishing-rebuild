Where to Find Us

West Ocean City, behind the Shark

12924 Sunset Ave
Ocean City, MD 21842

The boat is located at West Ocean City behind the Shark Restaurant. If you use GPS the address is 12924 Sunset Ave., Ocean City, MD 21842.

We sail from the Shark Restaurant. There is no parking in their lot — loading and unloading only at the boat. But there is plenty of free parking on the street and 200 ft away on the very large public lot.

When you turn on Sunset Ave from Golf Course Road, the entire block on the left you can park free on the grass, which is Worcester County Parking. There is a huge Worcester County free parking lot and boat ramp on the right, where anybody can park cars, boat trailers, RVs, etc. About 150 ft away from our dock. So there is absolutely no parking problem here. Just no commercial trucks are allowed.
